Appendix · Under the hood

Thirteen tools, one voice path.

Memory
  • get_contextProfile, memory, people, and upcoming occasions with budgets and arrive-by dates.
  • rememberSaves a durable fact about you or someone you shop for.
Discover
  • search_booksGoogle Books + Open Library: covers, ISBNs, verified hardcover or paperback.
  • web_searchRetailer search with a Tavily key; otherwise Codex’s own live web search.
  • read_pageOpens a product page to confirm price, stock and the delivery estimate.
  • find_game_dealsCheapShark: current deals across Steam, GOG, Epic, Humble and more.
  • search_used_hardwareSecond-hand listings from r/homelabsales and r/hardwareswap.
Decide
  • check_offersDeterministic: total, expected arrival, spare days, verdict and rank.
Show
  • show_*show_products, show_recommendation and show_question render cards in the app.
Act
  • save_to_planSaves the chosen item to your plan for the occasion.
  • create_watchStarts a deal watch; watchers re-check it and post to Today.

Voice path

An Agora ConvoAI agent joins the RTC channel (Agora ASR, turn-taking). Its LLM is “custom”, OpenAI-style: our /v1/sessions/:id/chat/completions over a public tunnel. TTS: Murf FALCON, streamed.

Never quiet

“On it” at 1.5 s. Each real tool step is narrated as it starts, at least 7 s apart. A filler after 16 s of quiet. The answer streams sentence by sentence; if you talk over it, the run keeps going.

Codex

codex exec --json. One thread per conversation, resumed every turn. Read-only sandbox, no approvals, live web search on.

App

Expo SDK 58, React Native 0.88. react-native-agora on Android, agora-rtc-sdk-ng on web. Polls each run for live steps and cards.

Watchers

Every 30 minutes: re-check game deals and used hardware, nudge upcoming occasions, post to Today. /api/tts reads any card aloud with Murf.

Appendix · What’s real

What’s live, what’s seeded.

Live

  • The voice call: Agora ConvoAI and Murf Falcon
  • Every search: book catalogues, retailer pages, game stores
  • Prices and delivery dates, as each retailer states them
  • Covers, game deals and the step-by-step narration
  • Radar’s scheduled price checks

Seeded

  • Your profile
  • People, and what Xerxes remembers about them
  • Occasions, budgets and arrive-by dates (you can add and edit your own)
  • The starting watch list on Radar

Not yet

  • No checkout: Buy opens the retailer, and you pay there
  • Delivery dates are the retailer’s estimate, or a typical window flagged “estimated”
  • Voice needs a public tunnel to the backend
  • Used-hardware search needs Reddit API access
